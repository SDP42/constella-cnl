# Playbook: Smart Classroom
Target: schools, colleges, state education tenders, system integrators serving education.
Bill of materials: interactive flat panel (ViewSonic or Samsung) per classroom, board-side mini PC (MSI), Honeywell structured cabling for the classroom backbone, Honeywell HDMI/USB-C connectivity hubs, optional ViewSonic projector for auditoriums.
Annuity: display and AV AMC per panel per year, annual cabling certification audit.
Timing: education budgets release March to June; state tenders close quarterly.
Talk track: "One PO, one point of contact for display, compute, cabling and AMC. Honeywell cabling is certified, so the school passes its network audit first time."
Objection - price vs Redington or Ingram: compete on bundle (cabling + AMC) not panel price; offer DaaS when budget is capex-constrained.
