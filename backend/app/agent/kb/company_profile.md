# Creative Newtech profile (from public investor material, Q1 FY26)
Founded 1992 in Mumbai (formerly Creative Peripherals and Distribution). Chairman and Managing Director: Ketan Patel. 31+ branches, 10,000+ channel partners, 25+ brands, 5,000+ products.
Segments: Enterprise Business (about 68% of revenue), FMSG social-media gadgets, FMCT consumer technology, FMEG electronics goods. Exclusive Honeywell brand licensee across 38 countries (structured cabling, connectivity, air purifiers, home audio). CyberPowerPC licensing.
FY25 consolidated revenue about Rs 1,774 crore, EBITDA margin about 4%, PAT margin about 3%. Strategy ranks products into quadrants by margin and working-capital cycle and moves resources to Quadrant 1 (high margin, quick cycle).
