# Deal shaping and credit policy
Creative Newtech operates at roughly 3 to 4 percent EBITDA, so working capital matters as much as margin. Rank deals by value-at-stake: probability times gross margin times cash velocity, plus three years of annuity margin.
If a deal exceeds the partner's credit headroom, do not drop it. Offer either a phased delivery in tranches that each fit the headroom, or Device-as-a-Service: an NBFC partner funds the hardware at about 14.5% per annum over 36 months, the partner pays a monthly fee covering hardware plus AMC/CMS/warranty, Creative Newtech is paid on delivery and keeps the service annuity.
Never extend credit beyond the approved limit without credit-control approval. Price-match requests above 3% discount need sales head approval.
