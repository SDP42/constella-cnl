# Playbook: Healthy Workplace (clean air)
Target: IT/ITES offices, BFSI branches, hospitals, schools in North and East India.
Products: Honeywell air purifiers, one per roughly 400 sq ft; optional AQI display monitors.
Annuity: filter subscription every 6 months; this is the highest-margin, near-zero working-capital line (around 34% margin).
Timing: demand peaks October to January with winter smog in Delhi NCR, Punjab, UP, Bihar and Kolkata.
Talk track: "Clean air you never have to think about — filters arrive before they are due and are invoiced automatically."
