# Routing contract and split credit
Every opportunity has exactly one lead rep and zero or more assists. The lead earns 70% of the credit on the closed value and assists share 30%. Account owners who are not the lead always receive a relationship assist, so sharing an account never costs the owner money.
Overlaps: if a new signal matches an open opportunity on the same account with any shared category, it is merged into that opportunity instead of creating a duplicate.
Escalation ladder: L1 nudge after 48 hours without acceptance, L2 reassignment to the next-best rep and division head notified, L3 national sales head review, L4 automatic digital nurture campaign sent to the partner.
Capacity: if the chosen rep is at capacity, the router borrows the least-loaded rep of the same division nationally; if every field rep is full, the automation-assisted annuity desk handles first touch.
